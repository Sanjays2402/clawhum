# Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

**Version: 2025-05-01**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement
between the customer ("Controller") and Clawhum ("Processor") under
which the Processor processes Personal Data on behalf of the
Controller.

## 1. Scope

This DPA applies to all Personal Data the Processor handles on the
Controller's behalf in connection with the Clawhum service, including
audio fingerprints, match queries, account metadata, and audit logs.

## 2. Roles

The Controller determines the purposes and means of processing. The
Processor processes Personal Data only on documented instructions
from the Controller, including with regard to international transfers.

## 3. Security

The Processor implements the technical and organisational measures
described in the Clawhum security documentation, including
encryption in transit, role-based access control, audit logging,
multi-factor authentication for administrators, and per-workspace
tenant isolation.

## 4. Sub-processors

A current list of sub-processors is maintained in the Clawhum
documentation. The Processor will give the Controller prior notice of
any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of
sub-processors.

## 5. Data subject rights

The Processor will assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligation
to respond to data subject requests under GDPR Articles 12 to 23,
including via the Clawhum privacy export and deletion endpoints.

## 6. Breach notification

The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay after
becoming aware of a personal data breach.

## 7. Audit

The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance with this DPA
no more than once per twelve months on reasonable prior notice.

## 8. Return or deletion

On termination of the underlying agreement the Processor will, at the
Controller's choice, return or delete all Personal Data, unless
applicable law requires continued storage.

## 9. International transfers

Where Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA, the parties rely
on the Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2 or 3 as applicable)
incorporated by reference.

## 10. Acceptance

Acceptance of this DPA is recorded per workspace via the Clawhum
admin console. The acceptance record includes the version string
above, the actor, the timestamp, the source IP, and the user agent.
The record is written to the workspace audit chain and is forensically
verifiable.
